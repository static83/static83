Good to know

What happens after I send my request?

Christa replies personally by text to confirm a day and time, and sends the studio address with your confirmation.

Are consultations really free?

Yes. A consultation is a relaxed, no-obligation chat about your hair, including a strand test for colour where it's needed.

How much will it cost?

Because every head of hair is different, pricing is confirmed at your consultation or before your appointment — never as a surprise on the day.

Is my information stored?

No. This form doesn't send or save anything. It simply writes a text message on your device, which you choose whether to send.
